## _Projects_

### My larger projects, more immediately relevant to my research interests and past academic work:

- [particles](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/particles), contextual
  particle frequency in written Japanese, taking a swing at the age old question
  of は vs が
- [thesis](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/thesis), the scripts and
  analyses from my graduate thesis work,
  ["Investigating Emotion-label and Emotion-laden Words in a Semantic Satiation Paradigm"](https://scholarworks.rit.edu/theses/10346/)
- [aozora_corpus](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/aozora_corpus), a
  compilation of Japanese texts pulled from 青空文庫, also available on
  [kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ryancahildebrandt/azbcorpus)
- [embs](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/embs), a project to provide
  tools streamlining sentence embedding or clustering techniques
- [siftr](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/siftr), a Shiny app using SIF
  sentence embeddings to separate out unwanted text data
- [priors](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/priors), an experiment
  combining pretrained and bag of words embeddings to incorporate prior semantic
  knowledge
- [bowts](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/bowts), an experiment combining
  pretrained and bag of words embedding approaches for embedding vector space
  manipulation
- [iterate](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/iterate), iterative
  clustering for sklearn clusterers
- [topics](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/topics), experiments and
  utilities for text topic extraction using decision trees
- [simsort](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/simsort), sorting texts by
  semantic similarity
- [nlt](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/nlt), representing tabular data
  in natural language
- [gsgf](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/gsgf), generate natural language
  expressions from JSGF
- [cluster_benchmarks](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/cluster_benchmarks),
  running multiple embedding/clustering workflows in task queues
- [c2g](https://codeberg.org/ryancahildebrandt/c2g), condensing text corpora to
  context free grammars
